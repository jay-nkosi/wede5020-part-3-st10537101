About Us

Get to know the story, purpose, and people behind the Jay Monate Auto SA

Our History

Jay Monate Auto SA is an exlusive South African pre-owned vehicle dealership established in 2015. This organisation was created to provide customers with luxury, reliable and affordable used vehicles while offering a professional buying experience.

The dealership initially operated from a physical showroom where customers could view available vehicles and speak directly with sales consultants. As more customers began searching for vehicles online, Jay Monate Auto SA identified the need for a professional website where customers could browse vehicles before visiting the dealership.

Our Mission

Jay Monate Auto SA's mission is to provide customers with quality pre-owned vehicles at competitive and exclusive prices while delivering transparent, reliable and professional customer service..

Our Vision

The vision of Jay Monate Auto SA is to become a trusted online and physical pre-owned vehicle dealership in South Africa by providing customers with a convenient, transparent and modern vehicle-buying experience. Having a close relationship with customers and becoming the number choice dealership in the country. Expanding the business by having multiple branches throughout the country. .
